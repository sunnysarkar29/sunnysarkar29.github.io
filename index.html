Recommended mastery path

  1. Model it. Lectures 1–3 → HW1–2 → Midterm Problems 1–3. Derive your ODE, equilibrium, Jacobian, poles and step response.
  2. Read it in frequency. Lectures 4–6 → HW3–5 → Midterm Problem 4. Derive Bode, Nyquist and Nichols from the same complex response; distinguish all disturbance paths.
  3. Prove stability and shape the loop. Lectures 7–9 → HW6–8 → Final Problems 2–4. Count encirclements, calculate margins, and derive a lead/lag/root-locus design.
  4. Verify the complete answer. Lecture 10 → Midterm Problem 5 → Final Problem 1 and extra credit. Check zeros, delay, saturation, model mismatch, and the entire transient, not just textbook rules.
